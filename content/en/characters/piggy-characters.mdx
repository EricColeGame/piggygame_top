### Audio and Visual Detection Rules

Experienced runners rely heavily on directional sound to avoid early elimination. Infected entities broadcast their presence through ambient themes, footstep rhythms, and jump-scare alerts:

- **Footstep Tempo:** Lightweight stalkers like Sheepy or Pandy take rapid, low-volume steps, making them deceptively easy to miss around tight corners.

- **Weapon Dragging:** Heavily equipped threats produce scraping sounds across flooring, providing early warning when rounding blind intersections.

## Advanced Gameplay Tactics Against Aggressive Piggy Characters

Surviving against player-controlled **piggy characters** requires vastly different strategies than escaping automated AI bots. Human hunters deploy tactical traps, bait narrow choke points, and memorize item spawns to intercept runners before doors are unlocked.

### Countering Bot Pathfinding
Automated bots navigate using node-based pathfinding. When an infected bot detects a survivor, it takes the shortest calculated walking route. Survivors can exploit this behavior:

1. **The Door Slam Stun:** Closing standard wooden doors directly in front of an approaching bot momentarily interrupts its pathfinding, buying 1 to 2 seconds of transition time.
2. **Table Looping:** Circling round dining tables or central pillars forces the bot into continuous turns, slowing its linear sprint speed.
3. **Crawlway Baiting:** Bots cannot enter standard floor vents. Forcing an AI bot to path toward a vent opening gives you safe passage into adjacent rooms.

```
Survivor Route:  [Hallway] ---> [Vent Opening] ===> [Safe Escape Room]
Bot Reaction:    [Hallway] ---> [Stops at Vent] ---> [Re-routes Long Way Around]
```

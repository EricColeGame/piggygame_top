## Who Is Mr. P? Identity, Design, and Evolution

Before the outbreak turned Lucella and its surrounding territories into infected ruins, Paul Poppyseed was a beloved public figure. Known originally as "Mr. Potato" during early development before transitioning to Paul Poppyseed, he built a massive entertainment fortune through carnival investments. Despite his vegetable appearance—specifically designed as a purple sweet potato—Paul was characterized by sophistication, donning a formal black suit, a red tie, a monocle, and a black fedora accented with a daisy.

As the narrative progresses and danger escalates, his appearance changes dramatically. Desperate to survive the infected hordes and defend military research installations, Paul undergoes extensive cybernetic enhancement. His sleek walking stick and monocle give way to mechanical prosthetics, most notably a plasma shotgun arm and reinforced metallic plating.

## The Tragic Backstory: Substance-128 and the Evalia Outbreak

Paul’s descent into tragedy began when his beloved wife, Piper Poppyseed, was diagnosed with a terminal condition known as Linnaeoma. Refusing to accept her fate, Paul directed over 40% of his entire personal fortune toward medical research facilities, eventually assembling a private team that included friends such as Bunny Bell and Shiloh Sheepy, alongside medical professionals like Dr. Beary.

Under intense pressure from Paul, the research team synthesized an experimental compound known as Substance-128. Although medical staff warned that the formula was volatile and insufficiently tested, Paul was determined to find a miracle. To evaluate the formula's safety, a local family volunteered: the Piggy family. While the youngest son, Georgie Piggy, was spared from the trials, the older family members consumed the mixture.

The initial results seemed miraculous, showing increased vitality in the volunteers. Overjoyed, Paul rushed the treatment to Piper. However, the side effects soon manifested with catastrophic results: the test subjects lost their sanity, developing glowing eyes and violent hostility. Unable to bring himself to harm his infected wife, Paul allowed her to escape, unwittingly sealing the fate of the entire region.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Piggy Mr P

### Why did Mr. P create the Piggy infection?
Paul Poppyseed never intended to create a virus. His wife, Piper, was dying from a rare, incurable condition called Linnaeoma. Paul funded private researchers to formulate Substance-128 as a desperate cure, but he rushed human trials on the Piggy family before the compound was stabilized, triggering the mutation.

### How do you unlock the True Ending with Piggy Mr P?
To unlock the True Ending with **piggy mr p**, players must complete specific prerequisites across Book 1 maps, including obtaining the Mrs. P photograph. In Chapter 12 (Plant), you must present the photograph to Mr. P during the final encounter, which triggers the complete cutscene where Georgie forgives him.

### Is Mr. P still alive after the events of Book 1?
In the True Ending, Paul Poppyseed's hat is notably absent from the floor, and community developer statements have indicated that Mr. P survived his stand at the Plant. While he does not appear actively in Book 2, his laboratory notes and narrative mentions confirm his continuing presence in the overarching lore.

### What weapon does Mr. P use during the Chapter 12 boss fight?
Mr. P utilizes an integrated mechanical plasma shotgun attached to his left arm. It fires glowing yellow energy spheres that stun players on contact, making him the first long-range projectile boss introduced in the game.

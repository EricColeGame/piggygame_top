## Build Mode Progression: How Creative Updates Reshaped the Meta

Build Mode is no longer just a secondary sandbox; it has become a primary avenue for player progression, community-hosted tournaments, and custom puzzle challenges. Early iterations of Build Mode were limited to simple obstacle placement, but subsequent **piggy updates** have added sophisticated scripting controls, bot customizers, and atmospheric tools.

One of the most consequential mechanics introduced to Build Mode is the **Allowed Abilities** setting. In earlier versions, map creators struggled to design tight stealth corridors because survivors or infected could bypass puzzles using unrestricted movement abilities. By allowing hosts to restrict specific abilities or traps per slot, custom maps now feature tightly tuned difficulty curves reminiscent of handcrafted official chapters.

Furthermore, creators can configure advanced logic setups using Fake Player NPCs and customizable fog sliders. By dialing in Fog Start and Fog End distances, builders can simulate claustrophobic sewer atmospheres or sprawling, low-visibility courtyards that test player routing under extreme conditions.

### Designing Better Puzzles with Linked Blocks

When crafting custom levels, rely on interactive blocks rather than static keycard hunts. The introduction of linked blocks allows you to build multi-stage extraction sequences. For example, triggering a switch can ignite a firework beacon across the compound, signaling to other survivors that a security gate is opening while alerting the roaming bot to your exact quadrant.

Community reports indicate that maps integrating these linked triggers retain significantly higher lobby engagement, as players must coordinate their escape efforts rather than sprinting independently toward exits.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Piggy Updates

### How frequently do new piggy updates release?
While major storyline chapters have reached their planned conclusions, the development team continues to publish regular **piggy updates** focusing on seasonal events, Build Mode expansions, engine-level bug fixes, and quality-of-life overhauls. Major feature patches typically arrive alongside community milestones or holiday celebrations.

### Do updates reset my unlocked skins or chapter progression?
No. All badges, chapter completions, unlocked traps, and skins remain permanently tied to your profile across all updates. When a patch modifies or refreshes a map (such as the House Refresh), your completion badges carry over seamlessly, though you may need to replay the chapter to unlock newly added secondary event badges.

### Why do some event skins disappear after an update?
Certain skins—such as those tied to winter quests, crossover events, or the Mansion chapter—are designed as time-limited rewards. When the event concludes, the quest triggers are disabled, making those skins unobtainable until the developer chooses to rerun the event in future **piggy updates**.

### Where can I test new Build Mode blocks before using them in live matches?
You can build and test custom layouts directly inside your personal Build Mode server slots. Hosting a private server allows you to test block linkages, fog parameters, and NPC pathfinding nodes without consuming tokens or risking your survival stats.

Keep the following survival techniques in mind during high-stakes matches:

*   **Avoid Narrow Dead-Ends**: Never enter a dead-end room with the power cell if the bot's location is unknown. If surprised, you have no escape vector.

*   **Audio Cue Recognition**: Listen for the telltale footsteps, music shifts, and mechanical hums. Dropping the cell to pick up a stun weapon is a valid defense if cornered.
*   **Coordinate with Teammates**: In multiplayer lobbies, have a teammate distract the bot or bait them away from the generator room before you attempt the installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Piggy Power Cell

### What should I do if the piggy power cell spawns in a dangerous location?
If community reports or your own scouting reveal that the cell is in a cramped basement or near a bot patrol loop, do not rush in immediately. Wait for the infected bot to patrol elsewhere, or have an ally draw the bot away with a distraction before retrieving the item.

### Can the bot move or destroy a piggy power cell that has been dropped?
No. In standard game mechanics, bots cannot interact with, pick up, or hide puzzle items. If you drop an item to pick up a defensive tool or navigate a vent, the item will stay exactly where it landed until another player picks it up.

### How do I know if a circuit requires a piggy power cell instead of a key?
Examine the interaction prompt on the locked obstacle. Mechanical padlocks display a colored key icon, whereas electrical panels, laser gates, and terminals usually display a battery silhouette, socket prompt, or wire terminal prompt indicating an energy item is needed.

### Why won't my piggy power cell slot into the generator in Build Mode?
In custom maps, the creator must properly configure the item receiver block to recognize the specific power item ID. If the cell will not insert, either a prerequisite trigger has not been completed, or the creator has not linked the receiver to that exact item profile.

You wake up inside an upstairs bedroom with a single goal: unlock the barricaded front door and escape before Penny corners you.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough to Beat Piggy Chapter 1

Completing **piggy chapter 1** smoothly requires executing tasks in a logical flow. Rushing down to the front entrance immediately is often fatal because Penny spawns right in front of the exit door when the countdown expires.

```
Wake Up (3rd Floor)
   │
   ▼
Grab Hammer / First Available Key
   │
   ▼
Clear Attic Gap with Wooden Plank
   │
   ▼
Use Wrench on Fuse Box to Cut Lasers
   │
   ▼
Gather Red & Green Gears for Backyard Shed
   │
   ▼
Retrieve White Key & Clear Front Door Boards
   │
   ▼
Unlock Padlock & Escape to Safety
```

### Phase 1: The Initial Scavenge (Upper Floors)

1. **Wake up and survey**: You begin in the upper bunk room. Immediately check the side dresser and adjacent hallway shelves for your first colored key or the Hammer.
2. **Access the vent system**: If you spot the Hammer, use it to remove the board blocking the crawlspace vent. This vent acts as a safe, cross-floor transit tunnel that Penny cannot traverse as quickly as players.
3. **Secure the Plank**: Search the bathroom shower or upper landings for the wooden plank. Carry it straight up into the attic and lay it across the open ceiling joists. This unlocks access to the attic's rear chamber, where critical keys and electrical panels spawn.

### Phase 2: Electrical and Utility Deactivation

1. **Grab the Wrench**: Once the attic path is open, grab the Wrench. Locate the glowing laser gate (often guarding the garage or an interior vault).
2. **Cut power**: Interact with the electrical wall panel in the attic or garage to shut down the security lasers. Disabling these lasers clears access to interior safes and the backyard walkway.
3. **Unlock secondary rooms**: Use the Red, Blue, and Green keys to clear every locked chamber on the second and third floors. This reveals the two mechanical cogs (Red Gear and Green Gear).

### Phase 3: The Backyard Machine and Final Escape

1. **Transport gears to the shed**: Head out the back door into the fenced yard. Enter the small wooden shed and slot both the Red Gear and the Green Gear into the mechanical dispenser.
2. **Acquire the White Key**: When both gears are inserted and the lever is pulled, the machine spits out the coveted White Key.
3. **Unbar the front door**: Return to the main foyer. Use the Hammer to pry off the remaining wooden boards, input the keypad combination retrieved from the safe, and slot the White Key into the central padlock.
4. **Step outside**: Walk straight through the unlocked double doors to trigger the victory cutscene and conclude the stage.

| Escape Requirement | Tool Needed | Location of Interaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Board Barrier** | Hammer | Front foyer entrance |
| **Laser Barrier** | Wrench | Attic or basement power switchboard |
| **Digital Code** | Code Paper | Keypad mounted next to the exit |
| **Final Padlock** | White Key | Front door lock cylinder |

## Survival Strategies: Evasion, Bot AI, and Movement

Surviving against Penny requires mastering spatial awareness and stamina management. Penny uses direct line-of-sight tracking and sound alerts. Whenever you drop down or sprint, nearby bots pivot toward your destination.

```
                  [Player Drops Down]
                           │
       ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
       ▼                                       ▼
 [Safe Landing]                       [Dangerous Stun]
 Crouch drop / clear stairs           Rail jumping / fence spam
 Smooth getaway                      Fall damage taken!
```

### Navigating Choke Points and Hazards

According to player experiences, many newcomers lose rounds due to misjudged jumps rather than the killer itself. The interior staircase features an invisible damage barrier if you vault off the top railing without landing properly.

| Hazard Area | Risk Level | Optimal Survival Tactic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Staircase** | High | Never jump over top rails; use stairs normally to avoid fall stun. |
| **Attic Joists** | Extreme | Place the plank before stepping forward; falling drops you to floor 3 with damage. |
| **Basement Stair Choke** | High | Check camera angles before descending; listen for footsteps. |
| **Vent Duct** | Low | Ideal loop path. Crawl through to break line-of-sight when pursued. |
| **Backyard Gate** | Medium | Keep camera rotated behind you to track if Penny follows you outdoors. |

### Bot Management Tips
- **Looping the Kitchen Island**: The rectangular dining table and kitchen island make exceptional loop structures. If Penny corners you on the ground floor, circle the table until she locks onto an outer path, then sprint toward the front doors.
- **Handgun Stun Windows**: If a teammate unlocks the purple safe, the Handgun provides a crucial 20-second incapacitation against Penny. Save your shots for moments when a teammate is trapped inside the basement or shed.
- **Trap Avoidance**: When playing against human Piggy users rather than bots, look out for bear traps laid at the foot of stairs, door frames, and crawlspace exits.

Originally inspired by indie survival titles like *Granny*, the House map was initially designed to be a standalone experience before massive popularity encouraged full episodic development.

### Evolution of the House Map

Community reports highlight numerous changes applied to the House over the years to balance gameplay:

1. **The Dining Table Adjustment**: Early builds featured an abnormally tall dining table that hid items underneath. It was later shortened to balance killer-survivor sightlines.
2. **Bot Transitions**: In early alpha versions, Little Brother served as the primary bot before Penny became the official canonical chaser for the house.
3. **April Fools Variants**: The map temporarily transformed into "Gurt's House" in 2021 and received an alternate "House.EXE" makeover in 2024.

## Pro Tips for Speedrunning House

If your goal is beating the map under record times, route efficiency matters more than raw speed:

- **Drop Items Strategically**: Do not hoard items across the map. If you grab the Wrench on the top floor, bring it down near the lasers before swapping to a key.
- **Designate Roles in Multiplayer**: Have one player manage the backyard gear station while another clears the planks and keypad in the foyer.
- **Bait the AI**: Send one teammate to purposefully draw Penny into the basement, freeing up the top floors for easy scavenging.

By chaining these steps together, clearing **piggy chapter 1** becomes an easy warm-up on your road through the rest of the campaign.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way to escape Piggy Chapter 1?
The fastest route involves opening the attic using the plank, disabling the laser security system with the wrench, running the gears to the backyard shed, and bringing the white key straight to the boarded front door. Coordinating with teammates to carry the hammer and code simultaneously cuts clear times down to under three minutes.

### Who is the monster in Piggy Chapter 1?
The canonical bot in Chapter 1 is Penny (the titular Piggy), wielding a wooden bat. In earlier pre-release versions of the game, Little Brother briefly functioned as the chaser before the cast was restructured.

### Where does the White Key spawn in the House map?
The White Key does not spawn freely on a shelf. You must locate both the Red Gear and the Green Gear, carry them to the shed in the backyard, and slot them into the machinery. Once both cogs are active, the machine dispenses the White Key.

### How do you avoid taking fall damage in House?
Avoid spamming the jump button over the railings on the second or third floors. The game features drop hitboxes that register sudden altitude drops as lethal or staggering damage. Always stick to the main stairways or safe drop-down ledges when changing floors.

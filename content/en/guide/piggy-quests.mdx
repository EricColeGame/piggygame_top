## Core Permanent Piggy Quests: Step-by-Step Walkthroughs

Permanent map skins can be unlocked at any point during the year. These puzzles challenge your spatial awareness and force you to preserve critical escape supplies for secondary objectives.

### 1. Poley (Mall)
Poley remains one of the most recognizable badge skins in the community. Unlocking him requires traversing the Mall while hoarding multiple keys to unlock the hidden security vault.

*   **Step 1:** Progress through the Mall until you have unlocked access to the upper levels and located the mirror, green key, orange key, and blue key.
*   **Step 2:** Rather than burning these keys exclusively on main exit barriers, bring the green, orange, and blue keys down to the bank vault area.
*   **Step 3:** Insert all three keys into their respective locks on the reinforced vault door.
*   **Step 4:** Once the vault opens, jump through the interior laser obstacle course. Reaching the end triggers the unlock sound.

### 2. Phenna (Temple)
The journey to unlock Phenna involves an ancient hieroglyphic puzzle tucked deep inside the Book 2 Temple.

*   **Step 1:** Fully unlock the doors throughout the Temple map so you have free movement.
*   **Step 2:** Grab the candle and locate the initial question mark drawn next to the purple safe. Ignite it using your candle.
*   **Step 3:** Search the map for four additional hidden question marks. Known locations include the blue door chamber, the hallway leading toward the red door, inside the red door room, and the approach path to the green door.
*   **Step 4:** Each time you light a question mark, look around that specific room for an ancient symbol that materializes. Note the exact design and the order in which you found it.
*   **Step 5:** Head to the primary bot spawn zone near the wall vent. Input the four symbols into the wall interface in the exact order discovered to finalize the unlock.

### 3. Laura (Camp)
Unlocking Laura requires steady nerves, as this objective must be attempted on Player mode while dodging human-controlled threats.

*   **Step 1:** Work through the Camp until the elevator shaft is accessible.
*   **Step 2:** Interact with the Insolence Eye resting near the main exit gate to officially kick off the mission.
*   **Step 3:** Locate six detached teddy bear parts scattered across the campgrounds.

| Item Spawn Point | Specific Landmark |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bridge Perimeter** | Along the outer edges of the wooden ravine bridge |
| **Main Tree** | Directly in front of the central Piggy spawn |
| **Grey Cabin** | Tucked against the bunk beds inside |
| **Purple Safe Tent** | Positioned beside the floor cot |
| **Blue Cabin Exterior** | Next to the snowdrift behind the tower cabin |
| **Green Cabin Interior** | Beside the nightstand near the red key watchtower |

*   **Step 4:** After assembling the bear, enter the blue cabin adjacent to the watchtower.
*   **Step 5:** Bait a secondary NPC enemy into the cabin directly in front of the teddy bear to register the unlock.

### 4. Anteo & Jakayla (Plant)
The Plant map contains multiple pathways for secret hunters. For Anteo, players must tackle Infection mode without consuming the chemical vials on the main escape door.

*   **Anteo's Recipe:** Lay the wooden plank over the chasm leading toward the dark laboratory corridor. Bring an orange vial and a purple vial into the chamber. Pour them into the laboratory cauldron, balance the color mixtures on the adjacent workbench, and pick up the resulting explosive device to claim the reward.
*   **Jakayla's Trial:** Detonate TNT in the lower orange wing to reveal a hidden vault guarded by multi-colored Sentinels. Use the crowbar on seven dull emeralds across the hammer tube room, align the central courtyard rings, shoot the locking collars with the handgun, and enter the alphabetical door code (`A-B-C-D-E-F-G-H`).

## Navigating Complex Multi-Portal and Subplace Quests

Some of the most demanding **piggy quests** require traveling between alternate dimensions or completing multi-tier combat trials under strict failure penalties.

### The Ombra Gauntlet (Breakout)
Ombra stands out as one of the most intricate challenges in Book 2. Survivors must locate the green wire by the storage tanks, wire the fuse box, and check the tactical chalkboard for randomized color coordinates. 

These coordinates correspond to four dimensional portals scattered around the compound:

| Subplace Dimension | Core Hazard | Objective Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tigry's Residence** | Red Ombra patrol | Evade the pursuer, retrieve house keys, record Door Code #1 |
| **Chasing Hand Maze** | Giant grasping entity | Navigate tight corridors to the safe room for Door Code #2 |
| **School Classroom** | Floor hands / Purple Ombra | Push colored desks to matching tiles to reveal Door Code #3 |
| **Military Boot Camp** | Sniper fire / Turrets | Clear the obstacle course without taking hits for Door Code #4 |

Once all four numerical digits are compiled, return to the main armory, input the combination, retrieve the military combat knife, and strike Ombra to conclude the trial.

### Marley Russell (Lab)
For this Book 2 finale trial, enter Lab on any standard ruleset except Traitor. Progress through the orange card security checkpoint and retrieve the revolver from the office drawers. 

Do not proceed into the secondary sector immediately; instead, preserve the five rounds of ammunition. Trigger the Sentinel security squad, eliminate each Sentinel alongside the primary infected bot, and hand the discharged firearm to Willow before completing the map.

## Seasonal and Time-Limited Piggy Quests

Many fan-favorite characters are tied to seasonal holiday updates. While these events are only active during specific months, mastering their mechanics ensures you are prepared when the developer rotates content back into play.

```
Annual Quest Schedule:
├── Spring (Easter Period): Bess Egg Hunt across Book 2
├── Autumn (October): Pumpiggy, Crove, Owell, Bakari, Bela
└── Winter (Dec - Jan): Mr. Bliss Snowman, Primrose Rescue, Holiday Specials
```

### Autumn and Halloween Challenges
During October, Mansion and Store become hotspots for secret hunters:
*   **Pumpiggy (Mansion):** Interact with every orange tome in the library, then scour the estate for five hidden Pig-o'-Lanterns (commonly hidden in the laundry room, kitchen, balcony, or portal bedroom) before slotting the books back into the shelves.
*   **Owell (Store):** Place a ladder in the crate storage room, use scissors to slice a feeding vent into the red wooden container, insert fresh grass, and collect the dropped grimoire from GorillAirways or the orange key room.
*   **Bela (Mansion):** Carry an item while bumping into six Pig-o'-Lanterns across the estate to harvest ritual candles. Light all six in the basement portal room, then steal the black key directly off Duocara's back while utilizing camouflage or waiting for his passive blue-face cycle.

### Winter and Holiday Challenges
December brings specialized weather puzzles to maps like Refinery:
*   **Mr. Bliss (Refinery):** Peer underneath parked vehicles with your third-person camera to locate the utility shovel. Clear snow drifts across the exterior yard to uncover the buried top hat, rummage through second-floor crates for the scarf, and dress the spawn snowman. Feed the snowman a fresh carrot before anyone uses it on the map's regular escape puzzles.
*   **Primrose (Refinery):** Locate the locked cell door beyond the orange sector. Fire a sidearm at the reinforced iron gate to trigger dialogue with the trapped inmate, then slip a screwdriver through the bars to earn their hidden gift.

## Essential Survival Tips for Quest Hunters

Undertaking custom trials while evading aggressive monsters requires a different playstyle than casual speedrunning. Implementing these tactical adjustments will drastically increase your success rate:

1.  **Deploy Crowd Control Distractions:** When attempting multi-step item hunts like Laura or Bela, designate one teammate to kite the primary infected using tight loop routes around tables and central stairwells.
2.  **Preserve Dual-Purpose Tools:** Certain items, like the handgun, carrot, or wrench, are required for both basic chapter progression and secret missions. Always confirm which puzzle needs the tool first to prevent soft-locking your attempt.
3.  **Adjust Camera Angles for Hidden Spawns:** Many mission items spawn below furniture or deep inside ventilation shafts. Lower your camera sensitivity and inspect floor clearances beneath beds, supply crates, and vehicle chassis.
4.  **Confirm the Audio Cue:** Never leave a match prematurely. Wait until you hear the distinct cash register chime before heading to the escape gate or exiting to the main menu.

By systematically working through these **piggy quests**, you can master the game's deepest lore puzzles and unlock some of the rarest cosmetics in survival horror history.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Piggy Quests

### Can I complete piggy quests in VIP or private servers?
Yes, private servers are the recommended environment for finishing **piggy quests**. Playing in a private match allows you and your team to control the map selection, prevent random players from escaping prematurely, and communicate without interference while solving complex item hunts.

### What should I do if an item required for a quest does not spawn?
Most items tied to skin missions possess multiple randomized spawn points across the map. If an item like the gold key in House or a candle in Mansion seems missing, thoroughly inspect secondary locations such as ventilation ducts, sink basins, and open supply crates. If a tool was mistakenly consumed on a standard door puzzle, you will need to restart the round.

### Are all secret skin quests available year-round?
No. While core skins such as Poley, Phenna, Laura, and Anteo can be unlocked at any time, seasonal event skins like Pumpiggy, Mr. Bliss, and Bela only become accessible when their respective holiday updates or thematic map modes are actively enabled by the developer.

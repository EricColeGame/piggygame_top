Unlocking the **piggy hidden ending** represents the ultimate test of puzzle-solving, survival skill, and dedication in MiniToon's acclaimed episodic survival game. As the definitive canon conclusion to Book 2 and the overarching narrative, reaching this secret sequence requires meticulous preparation across multiple chapters. If you want to successfully experience the **piggy hidden ending**, you cannot simply walk into Chapter 12 and hope for the best. 

Every player in your group must follow an exact sequence of steps across Docks, Camp, and Lab to trigger the final confrontation with TIO. This comprehensive guide details every requirement, puzzle solution, and combat strategy needed to defeat the Insolence once and for all.

## Essential Prerequisites and Server Setup

Before starting your run, your team must meet strict criteria. If even a single prerequisite is missed, the triggers in subsequent chapters will fail to spawn, forcing you to restart the entire sequence from scratch.

First and foremost, at least one player in your server must possess every single story badge from Book 1 and Book 2. This includes all chapter completion badges, the True Ending from Plant, and both the Savior and Survivor endings from Factory and Lab. Event badges (such as holiday skins) and the standalone Secret Badge are not required.

Furthermore, every qualifying player must enter their game settings and toggle the **Savior Ending** option to "On." Community reports confirm that if a player with all badges forgets to enable this setting, the secret keypad in Chapter 11 will never generate.

Because server hopping or disconnecting resets your journey immediately, you should execute this entire run inside a private server. Public servers introduce random map voting, accidental bot interruptions, and unpredictable teammate behavior that can disrupt delicate puzzle steps. You can set up your session directly through the [official Roblox Piggy game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/4623386862/Piggy) before gathering your squad.

## Phase 1: Preparing the Sequence in Docks and Camp

Phase 1 establishes the story state across two distinct maps: Chapter 9 (Docks) and Chapter 11 (Camp). Once completed successfully, you will not need to repeat Phase 1 unless every player leaves the server.

### Step 1: Docks (Chapter 9)

Select **Docks - Chapter 9**. While any game mode technically functions, community testing proves that **Player** mode (or Infection mode with an agreed-upon single infected player) is far safer than Bot mode because the AI bot tends to teleport unpredictably.

Your primary objective is to complete the entire map without Pony ever swinging his wooden sword at an infected enemy. If Pony attacks even once, or if you accidentally trigger Umbra, the sequence invalidates. The player holding all story badges must guide Pony through the exit gate. In groups of three or more, have all other survivors escape first, leaving the badge carrier and Pony to step through the exit last.

### Step 2: Camp (Chapter 11)

Next, vote for **Camp - Chapter 11** and select **Swarm** mode. You do not need to finish this map normally. Instead, your objective is strictly focused on a hidden lock:

1. Locate and collect the **Blue Key**.
2. Climb to the top of the watchtower and unlock the **Blue Key Door**.
3. Inside, verify that the secret keypad appears on the wall.
4. Input the code `467854` into the keypad.
5. Once accepted, your team can either escape or intentionally let the infected eliminate you. Dying saves substantial time and does not reset progress.

| Chapter | Required Mode | Primary Objective | Critical Failure Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Docks (Ch. 9)** | Player or Infection | Escape with Pony following qualified player | Pony attacks infected; Umbra triggered |
| **Camp (Ch. 11)** | Swarm | Unlock Blue Key tower door; enter keypad code | Missing keypad (caused by Docks error) |

## Phase 2, Step 1: Solving the Lab Chapter 12 Keypad

With Phase 1 registered by the server, vote for **Lab - Chapter 12** on **Bot** mode. Do not attempt this on Player mode; the bot is mandatory for the trigger to register properly. Assign one player to actively kite and distract the bot while your puzzle solvers work.

### Finding the Vents and Deciphering the Code

Upon spawning, look down the left hallway to locate a specialized keypad displaying strange glyphs. To unlock this keypad, you must find four metal vent covers scattered throughout the facility and pry them open using the **Wrench**.

**Important Note:** You must unscrew all four vent covers before entering any values. Removing a vent reveals a hidden number. The symbol order on the keypad and the numbers revealed behind the vents randomize every match. 

Player experience shows that mapping symbols to letters allows teams to quickly communicate the required combination. You must input the letters corresponding to the vent numbers in order from the closest vent to the farthest vent relative to the keypad.

| Vent Location | Distance Priority | Task Required | Success Indicator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vent 1 (Closest)** | 1st Digit | Unscrew with Wrench | Number revealed on inner wall |
| **Vent 2 (Mid-Close)** | 2nd Digit | Unscrew with Wrench | Number revealed on inner wall |
| **Vent 3 (Mid-Far)** | 3rd Digit | Unscrew with Wrench | Number revealed on inner wall |
| **Vent 4 (Farthest)** | 4th Digit | Unscrew with Wrench | Number revealed on inner wall |

If you enter the code correctly, the screen flashes **"PASSCODE ACCEPTED"** and teleports your team into the distorted dimension. If you make an error, the screen displays **"SYSTEM LOCKED"**, and you must restart Chapter 12 from the beginning.

## Phase 2, Step 2: Surviving the TIO Boss Encounter

Once inside the distortion, abilities remain disabled. You now enter the true climax required for the **piggy hidden ending**, facing TIO (The Iniquitous One) across two intense gameplay segments.

### Part 1: The Distorted Puzzle Gauntlet

Players have roughly 1 minute and 48 seconds to navigate a linear series of puzzle chambers inspired by previous chapters:

1. **The Alleys Keypad:** Read the numbers on the walls and punch the matching sequence into the door lock.
2. **The Ship Color Pillars:** Examine the left and right walls. Input the colors matching the roman numerals ($1 = \text{I}$, $2 = \text{II}$, $3 = \text{III}$, $4 = \text{IV}$) following the directional arrows.
3. **The Temple Circle Dial:** Align the rotating rings so the symbols match the wall markings, ensuring the black pointer aligns inward toward the center.

A common tactic discovered through community reports involves the "TIO Stall." When TIO catches an allied player, his jumpscare animation locks him in place for roughly five seconds. Agile players can slip behind him during this window, allowing them to solve puzzles unimpeded while TIO slowly advances forward.

### Part 2: The Memory Arena and Defeating TIO

After clearing the puzzles, you are teleported to a distorted castle arena representing TIO's childhood memories. This battle consists of two distinct floors.

On the ground floor, search the perimeter for three key items and deliver them to their respective allies trapped in hallucinations:
- **Wooden Sword** $\rightarrow$ Give to **Pony** (defeats the illusion of his father, Peter).
- **Pipe** $\rightarrow$ Give to **Zee** (breaks her trance facing an infected Zizzy).
- **Baseball Bat** $\rightarrow$ Give to **Georgie** (saves him from falling toward Penny).

Delivering all three items triggers the track *"Let Go"* and sends TIO into an immediate **rage state**. Unlike standard Piggy AI, TIO now possesses global awareness and sprints directly toward the nearest player.

Quickly run upstairs to the second level, which features distorted sections of Gallery, Hospital, and Mall. Here, you must find three final offerings to strike TIO:
- Hand the **Bone** to **Doggy**.
- Hand the **Carrot** to **Bunny**.
- Hand the **Grass** to **Zizzy**.

Each item delivered commands a fallen friend to strike TIO, dealing one hit. While searching the upper floor, beware of the White Sentinel and the **Shadow Sentinel**. The Shadow Sentinel moves faster and is nearly invisible in dim corridors, making situational awareness critical.

| Boss Phase | Item to Find | Target NPC Recipient | Boss State / Hazard |

| **Ground Floor** | Wooden Sword | Pony | Standard TIO patrol |
| **Ground Floor** | Metal Pipe | Zee | Insolence and Spike traps |

Once Zizzy delivers the third strike, TIO collapses, breaking the Insolence's grip and triggering the extended canonical finale.

## Narrative Impact: What Happens in the Canon Ending?

Securing the **piggy hidden ending** provides the true narrative resolution to the Piggy saga. Upon defeating TIO in his mental domain, the player awakens to find Sgt. Monroe and the military rescue force neutralizing the remaining infected horde outside the lab.

Willow and Tigry reach an emotional reconciliation. Acknowledging the cycle of revenge that cost them their loved ones, Willow relinquishes her bitter vendetta. Because their checkered criminal and military pasts prevent them from returning to civilization, the two depart together to explore the world on their own terms.

The post-credits scene cuts to a peaceful gathering at Kona's residence. The cure proves effective: Zizzy is shown fully restored to her uninfected self, joking with Pony about the high cost of carrots. However, as the camera slowly pulls away through an ominous, distorted doorway, the game hints that while TIO was driven back, the Insolence itself may still linger across the wasteland.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if someone without all badges joins our server?
As long as at least one player in the active server has earned every required Book 1 and Book 2 badge, has the Savior Ending toggled on, and leads the Docks escape, all other players in that match can participate and unlock the **piggy hidden ending** badge upon victory.

### Can you unlock the Piggy hidden ending on mobile devices?
Yes. The ending can be completed on mobile, console, or PC. However, mobile players often find the symbol keypad in Lab and dodging the rapid Shadow Sentinel upstairs more difficult due to touch controls. Adjusting camera sensitivity beforehand is highly recommended.

### Why did the keypad in Camp not show up even though we did Docks?
This almost always occurs because Pony struck an infected enemy during the Docks escape, someone triggered Umbra, or the badge carrier failed to escape with Pony right behind them. If the issue persists, complete Factory on Savior mode again to refresh your account data before re-attempting Docks.

### Does completing the hidden ending grant an exclusive reward?
Yes. Conquering TIO and witnessing the secret ending awards the exclusive "Completed Lab (Hidden Ending)" badge and unlocks the coveted **TIO Skin**, allowing you to play as The Insolent entity in custom matches.

## Prerequisites: Setting Up for the Hidden Ending

Before attempting this legendary finale, you cannot simply load into Chapter 12 and expect the secret sequence to activate. The game enforces strict account-wide milestones. If your squad does not meet these criteria before launching your run, the critical triggers will fail to appear.

### Account Badge and Server Requirements

At least one player in your server must possess every story badge from both Book 1 and Book 2. This includes every standard chapter escape, the Plant True Ending, the Factory Savior Ending, and all standard Lab outcomes. Event badges (such as seasonal crossovers) and the Secret Badge are exempt. 

Player experience indicates that running this sequence in a VIP server is practically mandatory. Random public server lobbies frequently disrupt your path by voting for unrelated maps or causing early game-overs.

| Prerequisite Element | Requirement Details | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Book 1 Badges** | Chapters 1 through 12 completed (including Plant True Ending) | Validates account completion history |
| **Book 2 Badges** | Chapters 1 through 12 completed (including Savior ending in Factory) | Ensures narrative progression checks trigger |
| **Savior Ending Toggle** | Must be switched to **ON** in game settings | Spawns keypads and dialogue flags in Camp and Lab |
| **Server Persistence** | All steps completed in the same server session | Leaving or disconnecting resets progress completely |
| **Carrying Player** | The player holding all badges must complete key objectives | Dictates whether map triggers open for the rest of the lobby |

Ensure every person in the party who holds all badges toggles the Savior Ending setting on before beginning Phase 1.

## Phase 1: Unlocking the Triggers in Docks and Camp

The road to the canon **piggy ending** begins across two earlier Book 2 chapters: Chapter 9 (Docks) and Chapter 11 (Camp). You must complete specific actions without causing hostile NPC interruptions.

```
[Docks: Chapter 9] ──(Escape with Pony, No Attacks)──> [Camp: Chapter 11] ──(Blue Key & Keypad 467854)──> [Phase 2 Ready]
```

### Step 1: Docks (Chapter 9)

Vote for **Docks - Chapter 9** on either Player or Infection mode. Infection with a cooperating friend acting as the single infected is ideal, because Bot mode introduces unpredictable bot pathfinding that can easily ruin the run.

1. Work through the map puzzles as normal to unlock the exit gates.
2. Ensure Pony **never** hits the infected monster with his wooden sword. Community reports highlight that if Pony swings his weapon or speaks about spotting Piggy, the secret check is immediately invalidated.
3. Keep the Umbra bonus skin inactive; community testing shows Umbra triggers can interfere with flag validation.
4. When escaping, the badge-holding player must leave **with Pony directly following them**. If you have teammates, let them escape first, leaving the badge-holder and Pony to exit together last.

### Step 2: Camp (Chapter 11)

Next, vote for **Camp - Chapter 11** on **Swarm mode**. Swarm mode introduces multiple infected bots roaming the terrain, so speed and evasive movement are critical.

1. Navigate the campground to locate and collect the **Blue Key**.
2. Climb the wooden watchtower to unlock the Blue Key Door.
3. Inside, locate the hidden wall keypad. Input the code **467854**. This six-digit sequence references lore notes discovered earlier in Book 2 ("The first is 4, and the second is 6").
4. Once the keypad confirms the entry with a chime, Phase 1 is locked in. You do not need to successfully escape Camp; dying immediately afterward still preserves your progress for the session.

| Map Step | Game Mode | Primary Objective | Critical Fail Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Docks (Ch. 9)** | Player / Infection | Escape while Pony follows badge holder | Pony attacks an infected; Umbra activated |
| **Camp (Ch. 11)** | Swarm | Unlock watchtower door; enter code `467854` | Entering code incorrectly or quitting the lobby |

## Phase 2, Part 1: Solving the Lab Vent Code

With the setup complete, select **Lab - Chapter 12** on **Bot mode**. Assign one teammate to loop the infected bot away from the primary hallways while the puzzle solvers execute the keypad sequence.

In the hallway immediately to the left of your spawn, you will spot an interactive keypad displaying strange runic symbols rather than conventional numbers. These symbols translate into specific alphanumeric inputs that scramble on every server attempt.

### Vent Locations and Decryption

Equip the wrench item and scour the facility for four metallic vent covers. Removing all four vents is mandatory; the game will not register keypad interactions until every grate has been unfastened.

| Vent Order | Relative Location | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vent 1** | Closest vent near main spawn wing | Unscrew with Wrench; note symbol |
| **Vent 2** | Central corridor connecting hall | Unscrew with Wrench; note symbol |
| **Vent 3** | Secondary lab chamber | Unscrew with Wrench; note symbol |
| **Vent 4** | Farthest perimeter vent | Unscrew with Wrench; note symbol |

Behind each vent lies a glyph corresponding to a digit. Record the digits ordered from the closest vent to the farthest vent relative to the starting keypad. Translate those digits to the matching runes, then enter the sequence. 

A correct entry displays **PASSCODE ACCEPTED** and plays a dramatic cutscene. If you enter an incorrect string, the monitor flashes **SYSTEM LOCKED**, which shuts the door on your attempt and requires a map restart.

## Phase 2, Part 2: Navigating the Distortion Hallway

Once the passcode clears, the party is pulled into the mindscape distortion controlled by TIO (The Iniquitous One), the overarching villain behind the infection. You are stripped of all ability perks and placed on a strict timer of approximately 1 minute and 48 seconds.

```
Spawn Room ──[Alleys Number Code]──> Ship Room ──[Color Sequence]──> Temple Room ──[Circle Dial Align]──> Boss Arena
```

1. **The Alleys Room**: Examine the digital lock on the security door. Input the displayed room code to release the magnetic seals.
2. **The Ship Room**: Inspect the colored markings along the walls. Note the Roman numeral indicators ($1 = \text{I}$, $2 = \text{II}$, $3 = \text{III}$, $4 = \text{IV}$). Input the colors in arrow sequence starting along the left wall, followed by the right wall.
3. **The Temple Room**: A circular dial mechanism sits in the center of the chamber. Rotate the concentric rings until the inner symbols align directly with the black arrow pointing inward, mirroring the wall glyphs.

During this hallway sprint, TIO roams behind the party. Player experience reveals a reliable bypass: if TIO strikes a player, his jump-scare animation locks him in place for roughly five seconds. Agile survivors can sprint past him during this animation window to complete puzzles unhindered.

## Phase 2, Part 3: The TIO Boss Battle

Surviving the puzzle corridor transports your team to a multi-tiered dream realm styled after a grand castle and familiar locations from the survivors' memories. Achieving this final **piggy ending** hinges on coordinated item delivery under heavy pressure.

### Stage 1: Ground Floor Memories

Distorted specters of past traumas torment your companions. You must locate three key objects hidden around the lower hall and deliver them to your allies to free their minds:

*   **Wooden Sword** $\rightarrow$ Deliver to **Pony** (distorted City) to fight off the specter of his infected father, Peter.
*   **Metal Pipe** $\rightarrow$ Deliver to **Zee** (distorted Safe Place) to protect her against the phantom of Zizzy.
*   **Baseball Bat** $\rightarrow$ Deliver to **Georgie** (distorted House) to save him from Penny's illusion.

Once all three items are delivered, the soundtrack shifts to "Let Go." At this moment, TIO enters a relentless rage state, actively pursuing players with increased speed regardless of line-of-sight. The upper staircase barrier dissolves, granting access to the second tier.

### Stage 2: Upper Floor Showdown

On the second floor, you flip the script by turning positive memories into offensive weapons. TIO requires three direct strikes to fall.

| Memory NPC | Item Required | Boss Impact | Hazard Warning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Doggy** | Bone | 1st Strike against TIO | White Sentinel patrol line |
| **Bunny** | Carrot | 2nd Strike against TIO | Insolence traps & tight corners |
| **Zizzy** | Grass bundle | Final Strike / Defeat | Shadow Sentinel (nearly invisible) |

While collecting and delivering these items, two deadly Sentinels stalk the hallways. The White Sentinel follows visible patrol loops, but the **Shadow Sentinel** blends almost seamlessly into dark corners and moves at higher velocity. Stay in well-lit lanes and maintain clear voice communication to alert your runners. Delivering the final grass bundle to Zizzy knocks TIO unconscious, shattering the illusion and triggering the credits.

## Storyline Breakdown: What the Canon Ending Reveals

The narrative payoff of this climax reshapes the overarching lore of the series on the [official Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com). When TIO brings the Player into the distortion, he reveals his broader motivation: he believes mortal free will is inherently destructive, citing Mr. P's catastrophic experiments as proof. TIO warns that producing a cure will spark a brutal global war among survivors desperate to rescue their own families.

The Player rejects this fatalistic nihilism, arguing that the cure represents genuine hope. Upon TIO's defeat, the group returns to reality to find Sgt. Monroe and the military detachment holding the line against the remaining infected hordes. 

Willow and Tigry resolve their bitter animosity. Willow admits her thirst for revenge blinded her to the people she cared about, and Tigry accepts her apology. The two decline to board the military transport, choosing instead to travel the country together to rebuild their lives. 

The epilogue takes place at Kona's residence, showcasing a restored, uninfected Zizzy relaxing alongside Pony and the children. However, as the camera pulls backward into the shadows, a faint Insolence eye motif lingers—confirming that while TIO fell, the cosmic malevolence has not been eradicated entirely.

## Troubleshooting and Pro Tips

Achieving this resolution demands near-flawless coordination. If your run stalls, review these common friction points compiled from community reports:

```
Issue: Keypad missing in Camp
├── Did you beat Docks without Pony attacking?
│   └── No ──> Replay Docks; keep Pony passive
└── Did badge holder leave with Pony?
    └── No ──> Replay Docks; let badge holder exit last
```

1. **Camp Keypad Won't Spawn**: This almost always stems from Pony accidentally aggroing onto an infected bot in Docks, or the badge holder leaving before Pony reached the exit zone. Re-run Docks carefully.
2. **Item Soft-Locks**: In older builds, dropping an item upon death could erase it. The game now respawns key items in their original locations if a carrier dies, so check item spawns if a runner is caught.
3. **Timer Expirations**: If your squad struggles with the puzzle hallway timer, assign designated specialists for the Alleys code, Ship colors, and Temple circle dial beforehand.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes the Hidden Ending different from the standard Piggy ending?
The standard Book 2 conclusion leaves multiple character fates ambiguous and bypasses the battle against the Insolence's mastermind. The Hidden Ending provides the canon **piggy ending**, reuniting Zizzy with her sisters, resolving Willow and Tigry's character arcs, and unlocking the TIO skin and completion badge.

### Can I unlock the ending in a public server?
While technically possible, attempting this in a public server is extremely difficult. Uncooperative players often vote for different maps, trigger Pony's attack animation in Docks, or press random keypad buttons in Lab. Using a free private server ensures total control over chapter selection and gamemodes.

### What happens if the badge-holding player disconnects during the run?
If the person who possesses all required Book 1 and Book 2 badges disconnects or leaves the server, the active session flags are reset. You must start over from Phase 1 (Docks Chapter 9) within a fresh lobby.

### Do event badges count toward the Hidden Ending prerequisites?
No. Seasonal event badges and secret challenge badges do not count toward your eligibility. You only need the twelve main chapter badges from Book 1, the twelve main chapter badges from Book 2, and the prerequisite branch endings (Plant True Ending and Factory Savior Ending).

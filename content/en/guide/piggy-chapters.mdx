## Essential Survival Strategies for Conquering Any Chapter

Succeeding across all **piggy chapters** requires more than quick sprinting. The game rewards spatial awareness, inventory discipline, and enemy manipulation. 

### 1. Master the Art of "Looping"
In almost every map, there are circular architectural features—such as the kitchen counter in House, the dinosaur skeleton in Gallery, or the center island in Port. 
* Never run in a straight line toward a dead-end room.
* Keep the infected behind you while circling around wide obstacles.
* Once the bot commits to a turn, sprint toward your target puzzle door.

### 2. Practice Strategic Item Staging
You can only carry one item at a time. Leaving high-value tools in remote corners forces unnecessary backtracking:
* Drop critical tools (such as the Wrench or Hammer) in central hubs or near the final exit.
* Call out item locations in team chat (e.g., "Red key dropped near cafeteria").
* Avoid hoarding keys you do not immediately intend to use.

### 3. Track Bot Audio Cues
Every infected bot plays an ominous musical theme that grows louder as they approach, accompanied by heavy footstep audio:
* Wear headphones to pinpoint whether the pursuer is on your floor or directly above/below you.
* Watch for door swings; an opening door around a corner signals an infected patrolling your corridor.

Understanding how common items function across various **piggy chapters** prevents wasted steps:

## Frequently Asked Questions About Piggy Chapters

### How many total Piggy chapters are there in the official game?
There are 24 main storyline chapters: 12 in Book 1 and 12 in Book 2. Additionally, the game features several bonus and spin-off maps, including Distorted Memory, Heist, Breakout, and seasonal event releases, bringing the total playable map pool well over 30 unique stages.

### Which Piggy chapters are the hardest to beat solo?
Book 2 Chapter 10 (Temple) and Book 2 Chapter 12 (Lab) are widely considered the hardest **piggy chapters** to complete solo. Both maps feature high puzzle complexity, tight navigation spaces, and aggressive AI bots that leave very little room for player error.

### Do I have to beat Book 1 chapters before playing Book 2?
No, you can play any chapter from either book in any order you choose. However, playing through the **piggy chapters** sequentially is strongly recommended if you want to understand the overarching storyline, lore notes, and character arcs.

### How do you unlock the True Ending in Piggy?
Unlocking the True Ending requires completing specific puzzle sequences across both books. In Book 1, you must finish Chapter 12 (Plant) under the "Badgy" ending while carrying Mrs. P's picture. In Book 2, you must collect hidden Savior badges and make specific dialogue choices during the finale in Chapter 12 (Lab).

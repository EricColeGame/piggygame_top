## Map Overview and Multi-Story Architecture

The house featured in Chapter 1 consists of five distinct vertical layers, connected through central staircases, secret crawlspaces, and outdoor perimeters. Understanding how these zones connect prevents you from getting cornered when the monster cuts off main corridors.

```
       [ Attic / Loft ]
              |
     [ 2nd Floor: Bedrooms & Bath ]
              |
 [ 1st Floor: Lobby, Kitchen & Dining ] === [ Backyard & Shed ]
              |
      [ Basement / Garage ]
```

### The Multi-Level Layout

*   **Attic Level:** A high-risk zone featuring exposed rafters, loose wooden floorboards, and crawl vents. Falling through incomplete floor sections triggers instant fall damage or drops you into dangerous lower corridors.
*   **Second Floor:** Contains the player spawn bedroom (equipped with bunk beds), a hallway bathroom, and multiple locked storage rooms. This area features tight corners that require clear sightlines.
*   **Ground Floor (First Floor):** The central hub of the residence. It houses the main foyer, front exit door, kitchen, living room, and dining room. The front entrance is heavily secured with multiple padlocks, boards, and an electrical keycard slot.
*   **Basement & Garage:** A dark subterranean sector connected by a steep basement stairway. Contains parking areas, utility cells, and access routes to backyard drainage.
*   **Backyard & Shed:** An exterior enclosure housing a locked tool shed, fenced perimeters, and side windows that look directly back into the ground-floor dining room.

| Zone / Floor | Key Rooms & Landmarks | Notable Hazards | Primary Utility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

Player experience highlights that looping Penny around the ground floor dining table is one of the safest stalling tactics in the game. The shortened dining table layout gives players clear visibility while keeping safe clearance from melee swings.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough to Escape the House

Escaping requires a systematic approach. Rushing the front door immediately will get you trapped, as Penny spawns directly in the foyer entrance shortly after the opening countdown ends. Follow this phase-based roadmap to unlock the exit methodically.

```
[Phase 1: Early Scavenge] ➔ [Phase 2: Clear Barriers] ➔ [Phase 3: Utility & Power] ➔ [Phase 4: Final Unlock]
```

### Phase 1: Early Scavenge and Safe Clearing

1.  **Clear the Spawn Bedroom:** As soon as you gain control of your character, inspect the bunk beds and dresser tops. Grab any accessible key immediately.
2.  **Monitor the Bot Spawn:** Penny spawns in the ground-floor lobby. Listen for footstep audio cues and door slams before descending the central staircase.
3.  **Unlock First-Tier Rooms:** Use the Red and Green keys early to unlock secondary storage chambers. This expands your running lanes and reveals tool spawns like the Wrench or Hammer.

### Phase 2: Removing Physical Obstacles

1.  **Deploy the Hammer:** Take the Hammer to the front entrance to pry loose the heavy wooden boards blocking the doorway. 
2.  **Open the Attic Vent:** Take the Hammer up to the top floor to break down the boarded vent wall. This opens a critical bypass route around the stairwell.
3.  **Bridge Rafter Gaps:** Collect the wooden plank and lay it across the broken floorboards in the attic. Crossing without the plank leads to severe falling damage or drops you right into Penny's path.

### Phase 3: Electrical Systems and Utility Unlocks

1.  **Deactivate the Lasers:** Bring the Wrench to the ground floor or basement terminal depending on active gate wiring. Interacting with the power panel deactivates the deadly red laser grid protecting the final key components.
2.  **Retrieve Defense Options:** If soloing or playing against an aggressive player Piggy, unlock the purple safe to secure the Handgun. Keep at least one round of ammunition handy for emergency stuns in tight basement corridors.

### Phase 4: Final Door Decryption

1.  **Coordinate Final Keys:** Ensure the Blue Key, Orange Key, and relevant access cards are brought to the front door sequence.
2.  **Clear the Doorstep:** Check corners around the lobby for hidden bear traps before making your final dash.
3.  **Trigger the Cutscene:** Interact with the final lock on the front door to exit into the driveway and trigger the concluding story cutscene.

## Evasion Tactics and Bot Mechanics: Surviving Penny

Penny's AI relies on linear pathfinding and line-of-sight tracking. However, her wide weapon hitbox and the layout of the **piggy house map** make tight corners lethal. To keep survival rates high, use intentional looping paths rather than sprinting in straight lines down dead-end corridors.

### Looping Routes and Safe Havens

*   **The Dining Room Table Loop:** The dining table offers continuous visibility. Keep Penny directly opposite your position. If she reverses direction, immediately pivot your movement counter-clockwise.
*   **The Vent Crouch Maneuver:** Penny cannot walk upright through small square crawl vents. Crawling through the attic vent or basement chutes buys valuable seconds while the AI re-routes around the entire floor plan.
*   **The Second-Floor Balcony Drop:** When trapped on the upper landing, jumping over the railing down to the ground floor foyer acts as a desperate escape. Be cautious: landing improperly triggers fall stun hitboxes that leave you vulnerable.

| Evasion Node | Structural Advantage | Risk Factor | Recommended Counter-Tactic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dining Table** | 360-degree clear sightlines | Trap placement on narrow sides | Jump over corner edges; avoid hugs |
| **Attic Vent** | Bot cannot enter crawlspace | Slow crawl speed leaves back exposed | Enter only with a 3-second lead |
| **Basement Stairs** | Direct access to garage loops | Extreme choke point at door frame | Bait swing before pushing past |
| **Backyard Shed** | Isolated loop with window exit | Dead-end interior if cornered inside | Grab item and exit immediately |

Community testing indicates that Penny's swing animation has a slight recovery delay. If she swings and misses due to a rapid directional fake, you gain roughly 1.5 seconds of free sprint time to clear tight doorways.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Piggy House Map

### Where does the gun spawn in the piggy house map?
The Handgun always spawns inside the purple locked safe. To access it, you must locate the Purple Key (commonly found in the bathroom, garage side room, or second-floor hallway dressers). Ammunition spawns separately on small side tables or basement shelves.

### Can Penny go through vents in Chapter 1?
No. The AI bot for Penny cannot enter crawl vents. When you enter a vent, her pathfinding script forces her to walk around through the nearest standard door or staircase, giving you ample time to complete puzzle tasks.

### How do I avoid fall damage in the attic?
To traverse the attic safely, locate the wooden plank (typically found in the basement or second-floor closets) and place it across the open gap in the rafters. Walking across bare beams without the plank risks slipping through the floor, which inflicts falling damage or drops you into dangerous ground-floor territory.

### Why is the dining table shorter than it was in early game versions?
During early development, the dining room table was taller to fit a Red Gear spawn directly underneath. MiniToon shortened the table and moved the spawn point to eliminate balance issues where players could exploit hitboxes against the Piggy bot.

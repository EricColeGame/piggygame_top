## Map Overview and Layout of Piggy Outpost

The Outpost is a sprawling, multi-tier military base designed with distinct industrial and barracks sectors. Unlike earlier, more claustrophobic maps such as House or Station, the **piggy outpost** combines broad outdoor courtyards with tightly packed indoor facilities. Surviving here requires knowing how to transition between open pathways and narrow hallways without getting cornered by patrolling threats.

The map is divided into several main areas:

*   **The Courtyard and Entrance:** Where players spawn, featuring barricades, watchtowers, and open dirt pathways.
*   **The Sleeping Quarters:** A multi-room barracks filled with bunk beds, benches, and crawl spaces.
*   **The Planning and Meeting Quarters:** Two-story strategic rooms containing whiteboards, stairwells, and secure safes.
*   **The Medibay:** A clinical treatment facility housing critical safes and medical equipment.
*   **The Cafeteria:** A dining and kitchen sector protected by a hazardous high-voltage laser gate.
*   **The Power Plant:** A concrete cylindrical structure at the back of the facility that serves as the final escape gate.

## Step-by-Step Piggy Outpost Escape Walkthrough

Completing the **piggy outpost** requires clearing multiple sequential puzzles involving power panels, vehicle logistics, and lock-and-key scavenging. Follow this optimized order of operations to escape efficiently.

### 1. Unlocking the Watchtowers and Upper Walkways

Start your run by scouring the central courtyard benches and ground-level rooms for the **Blue Key**. Once located, take it to the gym area to unlock the Blue Lock Doors guarding the stairwells. 

Heading up grants access to the high catwalks and skybridge. Here, you will typically locate either the **Wrench**, the **Orange Key**, or the **Green Key**. Grabbing the Wrench early is ideal, as it allows you to shut down the cafeteria laser gate and prepare the Power Plant exit panel simultaneously.

### 2. Clearing the Barracks and Medibay

Use the Orange Key on the sleeping quarters door. Inside the barracks, search the benches and crawl spaces to retrieve the **Red Key**. 

Next, head to the Planning Quarter entrance and unlock it with the Red Key. Scour underneath the interior staircase to locate the **Yellow Key**. Take this Yellow Key directly into the Medibay (unlocked with the Green Key) and open the Yellow Safe to secure the **Fire Extinguisher**.

### 3. Fueling and Arming the Military Tank

The tank parked near the center of the base is your ticket through the reinforced barrier. To operate it, complete two objectives:

1.  **Douse the Flames and Grab Gasoline:** Use the Fire Extinguisher to put out the fires near the Planning Quarter upper walkway and the Power Plant entrance. This reveals the **Gasoline**. Carry the gas to the back of the tank to fuel the engine, causing it to lurch forward and smash through the chained fence.
2.  **Retrieve and Load the Tank Shell:** Head inside the cafeteria kitchen. Ensure the laser gate has been disabled with your Wrench, then collect the **Tank Bullet** resting on the counter. Bring the shell back to the tank. The tank's turret will rotate toward the cracked brick wall and blast it open.

### 4. Opening the Power Plant Exit

With the cracked wall destroyed, enter the newly accessible Meeting Quarter. Behind the central conference table or whiteboard, you will find the **Blue Keycard**. 

Carry the Blue Keycard directly to the cylindrical Power Plant exit. If you have already removed the laser/electrical lock using the Wrench and extinguished the fire blocking the threshold, swipe the keycard to trigger the final escape cutscene.

## Surviving the Threats: Torcher and Infected Soldier Mechanics

The **piggy outpost** stands out in Book 1 as the first map to feature both a primary killer and an army of secondary enemies spawning dynamically throughout the match.

### Primary Threat: Torcher

Torcher is the canonical primary bot of Chapter 11. Equipped with a military gas mask and an industrial flamethrower, Torcher relentlessly tracks the nearest visible player. 

*   **Door Delay:** Torcher takes slightly longer to open doors than standard Piggy variants, giving you valuable seconds to turn tight corners.
*   **Stun Windows:** Feeding Pony a carrot causes him to charge Torcher, stunning the bot for a full 20 seconds. Alternatively, firing the map's handgun knocks Torcher out for the same duration. Remember that only three bullets spawn per match, so conserve ammunition for emergencies.

### Secondary Threat: The Soldier Swarm

Unlike primary killers, infected Soldiers do not pursue players across the entire map. Instead, they emerge from dirt holes scattered across open courtyards and wander around their spawn zones.

*   **Lethal Contact:** Walking into an infected Soldier causes an instant death, making spatial awareness crucial during high-speed chases.
*   **Zizzy’s Defense:** Zizzy acts as an automated ally. She patrols the map and automatically strikes down any active Soldiers she encounters, forcing them to burrow back underground.
*   **Cosmetic Interaction:** Community reports and player experience highlight an interesting easter egg: wearing the *Mask of Ghoulish Despair* skin causes Soldiers to temporarily freeze in fear when approached.

## Advanced Strategies for Solo and Speedrun Matches

Navigating the **piggy outpost** solo requires aggressive routing and strict inventory management. In multiplayer, teammates can distract Torcher, but in a solo run, you must manage bot aggro while carrying every single tool yourself.

### Route Optimization Tips

*   **Prioritize the Skybridge:** Never leave the Blue Key behind. Unlocking the upper gym early opens alternative escape routes, preventing you from being cornered in the lower courtyard.
*   **Pre-Place the Wrench:** Because the Wrench is required in two separate locations (the cafeteria and the exit), use it on the cafeteria laser panel first, grab the Tank Bullet, and then immediately hit the Power Plant panel on your way back.
*   **Ignore Optional Distractions:** While feeding Pony can keep Torcher off your back, community reports confirm that giving Pony a carrot is completely optional. Skilled players can kite Torcher through the bunk beds and save time by ignoring the carrots entirely.

## Lore, Secret Easter Eggs, and Chapter 11 Storyline

The story in Chapter 11 represents a pivotal turning point in the game's narrative. Players arrive at the military outpost alongside Zizzy and Pony after tracking a faint helicopter signal. Upon arrival, they discover the base completely overwhelmed, the garrison having succumbed to the infection despite their heavy defenses.

As the team prepares to breach the central plant, Georgie Pig makes an unexpected appearance, having trailed the group in search of the person who harmed his family. With a massive swarm of infected soldiers closing in, Zizzy and Pony heroically stay behind to hold the line, sending the Player and Georgie inside to confront Mr. P.

Inside the facility, players discover that Mr. P has undergone a radical transformation into a cyborg, replacing his arm with a mechanical cannon. Overcome with guilt and grief over his failed cure, Mr. P hallucinates, believing the Player and Georgie are vengeful ghosts come to haunt him before fleeing deeper into the plant.

### Notable Easter Eggs in Chapter 11

*   **Bunny's Ghost:** If you look closely at one of the distant watchtowers overlooking the courtyard, the translucent apparition of Bunny can be seen observing the player—a haunting reminder of the events in Chapter 6.
*   **The Decrypted Whiteboard:** The whiteboard inside the Meeting Room contains a substitution cipher that reads: *"I was attacked by one of them, and now I am here, wandering, just like them."* This confirms the tragic fate of the military personnel stationed at the base.
*   **Military Design Authenticity:** The tank model used to smash the barrier is modeled after the real-world M1 Abrams main battle tank, while the extraction helicopter is inspired by the AH-6 "Little Bird."

## Frequently Asked Questions About Piggy Outpost

### Is it mandatory to give Pony a carrot to beat the Piggy Outpost map?
No. Unlike several other chapters where companion NPCs must clear obstacles, giving a carrot to Pony in the **piggy outpost** is entirely optional. It only serves to stun Torcher or nearby soldiers for 20 seconds, which is helpful but not required to finish the escape sequence.

### How do you get past the laser gate in the cafeteria?
To bypass the laser grid blocking the cafeteria kitchen, locate the Wrench (typically found on the upper watchtower walkways or near the courtyard stairs) and use it on the electrical control box mounted beside the gate.

### What is the fastest way to deal with the infected soldiers?
The most reliable strategy is to lead them into Zizzy's patrol path. Zizzy will automatically attack any active soldiers, knocking them underground. If you have ammunition, you can also shoot them with the handgun, or simply maintain a wide berth around their spawn holes.

### Where does the tank shell go once you pick it up?
After retrieving the tank bullet from the cafeteria counter, carry it directly to the front barrel of the military tank. Loading the shell causes the tank to fire at the cracked wall, unlocking the Meeting Quarter where the final Blue Keycard is located.

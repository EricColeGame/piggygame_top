## Architectural Breakdown: Navigating the Piggy House Layout

The Piggy residence is a complex, five-level structure featuring verticality, hazardous falls, and interconnected utility rooms. Players wake up trapped on an upper floor bedroom, while Penny spawns directly in front of the main exit downstairs. Knowing the fastest transition routes between levels lets you lead the infected bot on wild goose chases while opening essential doorways.

The building is divided into several distinct sectors: the subterranean garage and basement, the main floor lobby and living spaces, the upper residential quarters, and the high-attic crawlspaces. Outside the back door lies a fenced yard containing a secure utility shed and outdoor seating.

## Step-by-Step Piggy House Escape Walkthrough

Escaping requires a clear division of labor or a strict solo route to avoid doubling back into the bot’s patrol radius. The front door is fortified by multiple interlocking locks: wooden boards requiring a hammer, a keycode lock, a master padlock, and an active laser barrier controlled by an electrical junction box.

You can experience this pulse-pounding challenge firsthand directly on the official [Piggy experience on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/4623386862/Piggy), where millions of players have tested their survival instincts.

The most reliable strategy involves working from the upper floors downward, clearing utility rooms before tackling the garage and basement.

| Step | Objective | Primary Tool / Key Required | Execution Details |

By following this sequence, you avoid unnecessary dead ends. If playing in multiplayer, have one runner bait the bot near the living room sofa while other survivors rapidly clear the basement and attic locks.

## Penny's AI Behavior, Traps, and Survival Tactics

In Chapter 1, Penny (the canonical bot) relies on line-of-sight tracking and audio detection. Surviving inside the narrow confines of the **piggy house** requires mastering looping circuits and using environmental shortcuts.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Piggy House

### How do you get past the laser gate in the piggy house?
To disable the laser security gate on the first floor, you must locate the wrench. The wrench typically spawns in the basement storage area, inside the yard shed, or on a side table. Once acquired, interact with the electrical junction box located nearby to power down the red laser beams, granting safe passage into the adjoining room.

### Where is the hammer used in the piggy house?
The hammer has two vital uses in Chapter 1. First, it is required to remove the wooden barricade planks nailed across the main front exit door. Second, players can use it to smash open wooden vent covers on the ground floor, creating safe escape shortcuts that the infected bot cannot quickly navigate.

### What is the safest way to escape Penny when cornered upstairs?
If Penny blocks the upper staircase on Level 3, do not attempt to squeeze past her hitbox. Instead, run into the bedroom or toward the attic drop-off point. Dropping through the floor gaps allows you to bypass the stairs entirely and land safely on the lower level, forcing Penny to waste time retracing her steps down the stairs.

### Why is Chapter 1 considered harder for beginners than Carnival?
While the **piggy house** is the introductory chapter, its multi-level verticality, narrow hallways, and dead-end rooms make it easy for beginners to get cornered. Maps like Carnival feature wide-open carnival grounds where kiting the bot in broad circles is much simpler than navigating tight domestic stairwells.

## Detailed Breakdown: How Every Mode Functions

Each mode alters how items must be gathered, how noise impacts your stealth, and how resources like stun weapons (planks, bones, wrenches, and firearms) should be prioritized.

```
       [ Match Start: 10-Minute Timer ]

         ▼                       ▼
   [ Standard Modes ]      [ Social / Betrayal ]
   ├── Bot                 ├── Traitor Mode
   ├── Player              └── Infection Mode
   ├── Player + Bot
   └── Swarm
```

### 1. Bot Mode
In Bot mode, the infected threat is controlled entirely by game scripts. The AI spawns after an initial 30-second grace period and patrols the map, reacting primarily to visual sightlines and player-generated sound cues (such as opening safes or stepping on squeaky floorboards).

*   **Behavior Patterns**: The bot walks along fixed waypoint grids until an active survivor crosses its vision cone.
*   **Predictability**: Experienced runners can easily loop the bot around dining tables, counters, and pillars.
*   **Trap Absence**: Classic AI bots do not place active bear traps, making movement corridors much safer to traverse.

### 2. Player Mode
Player mode randomly selects one person from the lobby to serve as the monster. Unlike programmed bots, a human Piggy can jump, anticipate shortcuts, guard high-value puzzle rooms, and set debilitating traps.

*   **Strategic Trap Placement**: Human hunters place bear traps in high-traffic choke points, such as doorways, bottom stairwells, and directly beside the main exit locks.
*   **Vision & Corner Peeking**: Players can swivel their third-person camera around corners to spot hiding survivors without revealing their full position.

### 3. Player + Bot Mode
This combination mode ratchets up the chaos by spawning both a human hunter and an automated stalker simultaneously. It tests a team's spatial awareness, as running away from a human opponent can quickly guide you right into the scripted AI's path.

### 4. Infection Mode
In Infection mode, a single player starts as the infected hunter. Whenever a survivor is hit, they do not enter spectator mode; instead, they immediately respawn as an infected ally with their own wooden bat or weapon. 

As the 10-minute timer ticks down, the balance of power cascades against the remaining survivors. Toward the late game, surviving often feels like an asymmetric battle against an entire room of pursuers.

### 5. Traitor Mode
Traitor mode introduces a social deduction element. The match spawns an AI Bot as the visible monster, but one survivor is secretly assigned the role of the Traitor. The Traitor receives a hidden knife after a set delay, allowing them to eliminate teammates silently while disguising their actions.

## Proven Survival Strategies for Every Piggy Game Match

Surviving any **piggy game** scenario requires tailored tactics. The strategies below highlight actionable maneuvers gathered from extensive player experience and top-tier community escape runs.

### Outsmarting the AI in Bot and Swarm Modes
When dealing with computer-driven enemies, exploit their line-of-sight algorithms:
1. **Master the Door Break Trick**: When an AI bot chases you through a doorway, quickly closing the door behind you forces the bot to play an opening animation, giving you 2 to 3 valuable seconds to create distance.
2. **Infinite Looping**: Identify circular structures—such as kitchen islands, decorative statues, or large vehicles. The AI strictly pursues via shortest pathing; by keeping the obstacle centered between your character and the bot, you can stall indefinitely while teammates clear puzzle rooms.
3. **Crouching Noise Discipline**: In levels with strict auditory triggers, crouching reduces movement sounds, allowing you to pass adjacent rooms without pulling bot aggro.

### Countering Traps in Player Mode
Human players rely heavily on surprise traps. Keep these counter-measures in mind:
*   **Angle Your Camera Down**: Never rush blindly through door frames or staircase thresholds. Point your camera downward to spot the metallic claws of a bear trap before stepping through.
*   **Trigger Disarming Safely**: If an aggressive hunter has barricaded an objective room with traps, coordinate with a teammate carrying a weapon or bone/carrot distraction to stun the player right as you purposely set off the trap, neutralizing both threats simultaneously.

```
Survivor Route: Check Chokepoints ──> Clear Traps with Teammate ──> Secure Key Item
                                          │
                                          └── Stun Hunter (Crossbow / Gun)
```

### Unmasking the Saboteur in Traitor Mode
Traitor matches hinge on observation and trust. Community reports and veteran guides recommend a strict protocol when navigating betrayal matches:
*   **Watch Item Hands**: Every legitimate survivor should actively carry a puzzle item (such as a keycard, wrench, or plank). If a player lingers empty-handed or hovers closely behind others in blind corners without contributing to locks, treat them with high suspicion.
*   **Control the Armory**: Ensure trusted teammates secure the gun and crossbow immediately. In Traitor mode, shooting an innocent survivor costs your team, but successfully shooting the Traitor removes the secondary threat for the remainder of the round.
*   **Never Corner Yourself Alone**: Always navigate in pairs. A Traitor thrives on picking off isolated players in dead-end basements or attics where scream animations go unnoticed.

## Custom Map Building: The Creative Dimension

Beyond the standard matchmaking queues, the community thrives through the built-in **Build Mode**. Players can craft custom horror layouts, design multi-layered logic gates, and select how their custom levels run.

Building an effective map requires balancing puzzle layout and monster pathing:
*   **Spawning Logic**: Placing survivor spawns too close to the monster cage leads to unfair round starts. Keep at least 60 studs of separation between spawn pads.
*   **Ventilation Shafts**: Vents create high-risk, high-reward shortcuts. Incorporating vents that require a wrench to open creates organic tension during high-speed chases.
*   **Lighting and Atmosphere**: Limiting ambient illumination elevates the suspense, making standard flashlights a meaningful survival tool rather than an aesthetic choice.

Whether testing custom challenges in sandbox mode or grinding chapter unlocks in official servers, refining your understanding of each mode's mechanics is the ultimate difference between escaping safely and falling victim to the beast.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which game mode is best for beginners in the piggy game?
Bot Mode is universally recommended for new players. Because the AI moves at a predictable pace and cannot lay strategic bear traps, it offers a forgiving environment where you can memorize map layouts, puzzle solutions, and item spawn locations without facing human mind games.

### How do you identify the Traitor before getting eliminated?
Keep a close watch on player inventory and behavior. Traitors often loiter near choke points, avoid holding functional escape items (like wrenches, keys, or gas cans), and shadow isolated survivors into dead ends. Furthermore, community reports highlight that Traitors will actively search for the hidden Traitor weapon spawn rather than helping with door progression.

### Can an infected survivor escape in Infection Mode?
No. Once a survivor is struck by an infected player in Infection mode, their win condition immediately flips. They respawn as an infected hunter and can only win the round if all remaining human survivors are tagged before the round timer expires.

### How long does an infected monster stay stunned when hit?
In standard play, hitting an infected player or AI bot with a loaded weapon (such as the gun or crossbow) or using a map-specific distraction NPC (like Doggy or Bunny) stuns the target for 20 seconds. This window provides ample time to pick up trapped keys, clear barricades, or slip away through a narrow corridor.

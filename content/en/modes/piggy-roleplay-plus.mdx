## Custom Storytelling, Server Types, and RP Etiquette

Like many community-driven sandbox spaces, joining a server requires an understanding of different roleplay expectations. Some lobbies welcome chaotic, casual fun, while others enforce structured, serious roleplaying rules.

Finding a server that matches your style ensures that your narrative efforts are rewarded rather than disrupted by off-topic antics.

| Server Type | Intended Audience | Expected Playstyle | Strictness Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Casual Freeform** | Beginners, casual explorers | Spontaneous interactions, morph testing | Low: Few rules beyond basic chat safety |
| **Semi-Literate RP** | Creative writers, casual storytellers | Multi-line dialogue, structured character arcs | Moderate: Requires respecting ongoing scenes |
| **Strict Lore-Based** | Dedicated fans, lore purists | Faithfully follows official Book 1 & 2 continuity | High: Non-canon morphs or actions may be discouraged |
| **AU (Alternate Universe)** | Creative communities, fanfiction writers | High-fantasy, sci-fi, or non-canon survival twists | Variable: Depends on the host's custom rules |

To maintain a positive environment in **piggy roleplay plus**, always respect ongoing scenes. If two players are engaged in a tense dialogue in the Safehouse, running between them with high-pitched sound effects disrupts the flow. Instead, introduce your character naturally by knocking on the door or observing from a distance.

### Golden Rules of Roleplay Etiquette

- **Avoid "Godmoding":** Never declare actions that force an outcome on another player without their consent (e.g., typing "instantly cures Piggy and saves the world" without letting the other player react).
- **Use Out-of-Character (OOC) Indicators:** When speaking as a player rather than your character, use brackets like `((Need to grab a snack, brb))` or `// heading to the lobby`.
- **Match the Tone:** Pay attention to whether the current scene is comedic, tragic, or suspenseful, and adjust your character's responses accordingly.
- **Give Others Room to Speak:** Avoid dominating the chat window with endless monologues; leave openings for other survivors to contribute.

## Performance Optimization and Interface Tips

Roleplay servers often contain dozens of detailed morphs, accessory meshes, and custom assets that can strain lower-end devices. If you experience frame drops while navigating crowded hubs, adjusting a few client settings can significantly improve your gameplay experience.

| Issue | Cause | Recommended Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Frame Rates (FPS Lag)** | High density of complex custom morphs | Lower graphics slider to level 3–4; disable global shadows |
| **Slow Asset Loading** | Large map textures streaming simultaneously | Allow 60 seconds in the spawn lobby for assets to cache |
| **Dialogue Getting Lost** | Fast-scrolling public chat | Use team chat channels or whisper commands (`/w [username]`) |
| **Accidental Morph Reset** | Pressing keybinds linked to avatar respawns | Rebind UI shortcuts or stick to on-screen button toggles |

By keeping performance smooth, you can focus on reading dialogue, enjoying custom animations, and immersing yourself in the survival narrative.

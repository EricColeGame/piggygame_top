## Staying Safe: Avoiding Scams and Phishing in Roblox Communities

Because Roblox communities attract millions of passionate young gamers, third-party platforms can sometimes become targets for bad actors. When participating in any **piggy discord**, maintaining strong digital security habits is essential to protect your Roblox account, personal data, and inventory.

Scammers frequently impersonate official moderators or automated verification bots. A common scam involves sending direct messages claiming you have won rare skins, exclusive badges, or free Robux, provided you click an external link to "claim" your prize. These links lead to convincing phishing pages designed to steal your account credentials and session cookies.

| Threat Category | Common Red Flag | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fake Verification Bots** | DMs asking you to paste code or enter your password | Only verify via public channels using official slash commands |
| **Phishing Giveaways** | Messages offering free Robux or exclusive skins via links | Block the sender immediately and notify server moderators |
| **Off-Site Trading** | Requests to buy or sell accounts and items for real currency | Decline immediately; this violates both Discord and Roblox Terms |
| **Malicious Downloads** | Files promising map exploits, skin unlockers, or coin generators | Never download `.exe` or `.scr` files from server members |

To keep your account secure, adjust your privacy settings to block direct messages from server members who are not on your friends list. Legitimate staff members will never ask for your account password, two-factor authentication codes, or session tokens.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find an official link to join the Piggy Discord?
You can find legitimate invites linked directly on the official Roblox game page under the social links section, on MiniToon's official social media profiles, or through verified community wikis. Avoid clicking unsolicited invite links sent via private messages to ensure you join the genuine server.

### Why do I need to verify my Roblox account when joining?
Verification links your Discord handle to your Roblox profile, creating accountability across the community. This process deters spammers, prevents banned users from evading moderation, and ensures that participants in community events receive their in-game rewards accurately.

### Can I get banned from the Piggy Discord for using exploits in-game?
Yes. Most community guidelines strictly prohibit exploiting, hacking, or abusing glitches in Roblox. If staff members receive verified evidence of you exploiting in public or private servers, you risk receiving a permanent ban from both the Discord server and community-run events.

### Where can I report bugs found in new chapters or Build Mode?
Navigate to the designated bug-reports forum or channel within the **piggy discord**. Ensure you follow the pinned format by including your platform, clear steps to reproduce the issue, and screenshot or video evidence showing the glitch in action.

## The Foundations of Evalia: Outbreak Origins and Mr. P's Mistake

The entire saga is set on the fictional continent of Evalia, a modern society populated by anthropomorphic citizens. Long before the player woke up in an abandoned house holding a flashlight, the seeds of the apocalypse were planted by grief and desperation. A wealthy industrialist named Mr. P (an anthropomorphic potato) sought a cure for his terminally ill wife, Mrs. P. 

Desperate for a breakthrough, Mr. P financed unauthorized medical experiments using an untested chemical compound labeled Substance-128. Believing human trials were the only way forward, he recruited the Pig family—Penny, Georgie, and their parents—to test the supposed cure. Tragically, Substance-128 did not cure disease; it mutated the subjects, stripping them of their free will, turning their eyes into glowing orbs, and instilling intense, violent aggression.

Penny was among the very first to succumb, transforming into the titular monster known to players. Georgie managed to escape before receiving the full dosage, sparking the detective investigation that kicks off the game's opening moments.

Community reports and environmental storytelling confirm that the outbreak quickly overwhelmed local emergency services. As the infection swept across Evalia's major settlements, civic structures collapsed within weeks, forcing survivors into militarized factions and desperate nomadic bands.

## Piggy Book 2 and The Silver Paw: Willow's Tragic Vengeance

If Book 1 is about uncovering the outbreak's origin, Book 2 shifts its lens toward human conflict, moral ambiguity, and the collapse of morality among survivors. Central to this narrative arc is Willow Wolf and her organized syndicate, The Silver Paw (TSP).

Long before the infection, Willow lived in poverty with her younger brother William in an alleyway hideout. After their parents were arrested by the police for stealing food to keep the family alive, Willow developed a deep-seated hatred for law enforcement. When the outbreak dismantled societal laws, Willow founded TSP, recruiting survivors and hoarding vital resources across the ruins of Evalia.

When TSP raids the Safe Place and infects Zizzy, the detective and Pony embark on a dangerous mission through alleys, stores, and refineries to secure an antidote and avenge their fallen comrade.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Piggy Lore

### Who is the real villain in the overarching narrative?
While Mr. P caused the physical outbreak through his flawed Substance-128 experiments, the true overarching antagonist in **piggy lore** is TIO, the physical embodiment of The Insolence. TIO manipulated key characters during their moments of grief, accelerating the societal collapse of Evalia.

### What is the timeline between Book 1 and Book 2?
Book 1 spans roughly from 2010 to 2012, charting the immediate months following the initial outbreak. Book 2 takes place shortly after the events at the Plant, detailing the ongoing survival efforts, the clash with The Silver Paw, and the search for an infection reversal cure over the subsequent months.

### Is Zizzy permanently cured in the canon ending?
In the standard ending, Zizzy's fate remains uncertain after being infected at the Safe Place. However, completing the True Hidden Ending of Book 2 reveals that severing TIO's control allows the survivors to stabilize Zizzy, offering hope that the infection can be halted or reversed.

### Why do the infected characters carry weapons?
Unlike traditional zombies, infected individuals retain fragments of their pre-infection muscle memory and personal trauma. Their weapons often reflect their former occupations or the items they held when they turned, making them tragic echoes of Evalia's fallen citizens rather than mindless monsters.

## Community Strategies: Mastering Maps and Evading Traps

Whether you are revisiting early chapters or trying your hand at user-created maps, fundamental survival mechanics remain consistent. Surviving in Piggy requires spatial awareness, efficient inventory management, and clear communication with teammates. 

As highlighted in recent **piggy news** roundups and community discussions, trap placement remains one of the deadliest tactics employed by skilled Piggy players. Below is an essential reference guide to item utility and routing priorities to help you survive tough matches.

| Item Category | Primary Example | Strategic Value | Recommended Player Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Access Keys** | Red / Blue / Green Keys | Unlocks doors to advance chapter progression | Call out locations immediately; do not hoard |
| **Disabling Tools** | Wrench / Wire Cutters | Deactivates electrical barriers and lasers | Prioritize early to open safe running paths |
| **Distraction Items** | Bone / Carrot / Grass | Temporarily distracts specific NPC threats | Save for tight bottlenecks or rescue situations |
| **Stun Weapons** | Crossbow / Gun | Temporarily incapacitates the chaser | Use only when cornered or saving a trapped ally |
| **Trap Counters** | Planks / Stepping Stones | Bridges gaps and bypasses floor traps | Clear routes before the chaser corners the team |

To consistently escape chapters, keep these proven player strategies in mind:

- **Communicate Item Drops:** When you pick up a key, drop it in a central, visible location if you cannot immediately reach its corresponding lock.
- **Bait Trap Placement:** Keep an eye on the chaser's movement. If you notice them placing a trap in a narrow doorway, find an alternate route or lure them into triggering their own cooldowns.
- **Conserve Stun Ammunition:** Never waste crossbow bolts or bullets on a chaser who is far away. Save your stuns for clutch saves when an ally is cornered.
- **Listen for Audio Cues:** Piggy relies heavily on directional audio. The distinctive sound of a door unlocking or a trap being set gives away critical positional information.
